Physical Model Making

Hand-made physical models of two residential projects designed and built by Cedrus. Every part was cut by hand from white foam board, cardboard, plexiglass, plastic sheet and balsa wood.

01. Sepinoud Residential Apartment ↓ 02. Villa No.2 Zibadasht ↓
Project 01

Sepinoud Residential Apartment

A model of a residential apartment building by Cedrus, made in 2023. The facade louvres were the most demanding part: each one is a strip of balsa wood, cut and fitted by hand into its bay. The model is lit from inside, so it can be shown the way the built building is photographed at night.

Typology Residential apartment building, Cedrus · 2023
Materials White foam board, cardboard, plexiglass, plastic sheet, balsa wood
Techniques Hand-cut and hand-assembled
