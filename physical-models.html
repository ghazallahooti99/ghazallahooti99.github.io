Project 01

Sepinoud Residential Apartment

A physical exploration of facade depth, private balcony stratification, and residential tectonic layering. The model was constructed to study natural daylight penetration into deep interior volumes and evaluate the material rhythm of the urban envelope.

Typology Residential Architecture Model
Materials Basswood, Greyboard, Clear Acrylic
Techniques Laser Cutting, Hand Assembly, Layering

Project 02

Villa No.2 Zibadasht

An architectural study investigating the dialogue between solid volumes, transparent garden thresholds, and expansive cantilevered roof planes. The model serves as an inquiry into indoor-outdoor continuity and landscape integration within the Zibadasht context.

Typology Single-Family Residential Villa
Materials Foam Board, Walnut Veneer, Cast Plaster
Techniques Precision Scoring, Cantilever Joinery
