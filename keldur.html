Keldur

Integrating Landscape Systems, Transit Infrastructure, and Urban Ecology in Reykjavík.

Typology Landscape & Transit-Oriented Urbanism
Location Keldur, Reykjavík, Iceland
Tools Used QGIS, Rhino, Illustrator, InDesign
Timeline 2024 – 2025

Site & Vision

Keldur is Reykjavík’s largest urban development frontier, planned as a premier transit-oriented development around the future Borgarlínan Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) spine.

The project addresses the challenge of building dense, human-scale communities in extreme sub-arctic climate conditions, where strong oceanic winds, low sun angles, and fragile volcanic-wetland ecosystems dictate spatial form.

Design Strategies

Bridging ecology with dense mobility networks:

1. Blue-Green Ecological Fingers: Preserving existing hydrology and peat wetlands as continuous natural biodiversity corridors.
2. Aerodynamic Building Clustering: Contoured massing configurations that deflect severe Atlantic gusts away from public courtyards.
3. Active Mobility Network: Continuous protected cycling and walking infrastructure centered on BRT stations.
