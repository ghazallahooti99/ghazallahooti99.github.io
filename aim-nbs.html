AIM-NBS

Advancing Impact Measurement of Nature-Based Solutions, at the Institute for Design Strategies, TH OWL.

Role Research Intern (two phases)
Institution IDS, TH OWL, Detmold, Germany
Method PRISMA Systematic Review, Indicator Extraction
Timeline Jun – Aug 2025 · Oct – Dec 2026

Research Scope

Nature-based solutions are widely promoted for climate adaptation, but the ways their impacts are measured are fragmented across disciplines. AIM-NBS reviews the scientific literature to identify which qualitative and quantitative indicators are used, across twelve societal challenges that NBS are expected to address.

My Role

Phase 1 · Jun – Aug 2025. The twelve challenges were split between two interns. I was responsible for six of them: I screened and analysed 410 papers following the PRISMA protocol, and extracted the qualitative and quantitative impact indicators used for each challenge.

Phase 2 · Oct – Dec 2026. I returned to the project to recalibrate the review findings for the final synthesis and to write the project report, in which I will be credited.

Related work: my Zotero to Google Sheets pipeline automates citation extraction for PRISMA reviews, and the evidence on NBS impacts is what UrbanSim's matching rules need to grow from.
