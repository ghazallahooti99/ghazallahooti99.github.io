Lindholmen

Sustainable Waterfront Transformation and Knowledge District in Gothenburg, Sweden.

Typology Waterfront Regeneration & Mixed-Use
Location Gothenburg, Sweden
Tools Used AutoCAD, Rhino, QGIS, Illustrator, Enscape
Timeline 2024

Industrial Heritage

Lindholmen, located on the northern bank of the Göta Älv river, is an iconic former shipyard now evolving into an innovation hub. However, its industrial legacy created vast mono-functional paved zones, physical barriers to water access, and wind-tunnel effects.

This studio project develops a comprehensive urban strategy to stitch together historic shipyard drydocks, academic buildings, and vibrant mixed-use residential quarters.

Waterfront Strategy

Reclaiming the industrial shoreline for civic life:

1. Active Quayside Promenade: Transforming former cargo docks into stepped public terraces, tidal pools, and public boat moorings.
2. Fine-Grained Permeability: Breaking massive shipbuilder footprints into human-scale urban blocks with sheltered courtyards.
3. Flood-Adaptive Edge: Incorporating sea-level rise resilience into tiered waterfront terraces.
