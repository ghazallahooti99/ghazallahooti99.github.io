Hi, I'm Ghazal

Urban Designer & Researcher · Urban Heat & Nature-Based Solutions

I’m an urban designer and researcher specialising in urban heat and nature-based solutions (NBS) in dense cities.

My work answers two questions at the start of the design process: where to cool first, based on where heat meets the places people walk to every day, and which NBS fit each street, given its width, orientation and existing uses. My MSc thesis answered them with satellite heat data, GIS, sun simulation and street surveys, and I am now turning that method into UrbanSim, a heat-screening tool for planners.

The approach transfers to other climate risks and user groups. I want to bring it into practice through transparent, data-driven tools, increasingly supported by AI.

Open to research and R&D roles in urban design and urban development.
